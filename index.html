About

Most of my work comes down to one question: can we trust this number? I like tracing a KPI back to its source, finding where it drifted, and fixing it so the dashboard means what people think it means.

At Accenture I spent three years doing exactly that. I worked with stakeholders to define KPIs, built the Power BI dashboards leadership used to track them, and ran the reconciliation and root cause analysis that kept those numbers reliable. Along the way I automated the manual parts of reporting with SQL and Python, which pulled me further upstream into ETL and data modeling.

In 2025 I moved to Charlotte for an MS in Computer Science with a Data Science concentration at UNC Charlotte, finishing in May 2026. Since then I've been building end-to-end data projects with dbt, DuckDB and Streamlit, and working with LLM tools in a way that matches how I treat any data source: useful, and always checked against the raw data.

I'm looking for Data Analyst or Data Engineer roles where I can own data from ingestion to the report someone opens on Monday morning.
